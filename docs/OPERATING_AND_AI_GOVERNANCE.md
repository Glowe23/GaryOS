# HELI-OS Operating Model and AI Governance

## Operating cycle

**Capture. Clarify. Prioritize. Plan. Execute. Review. Learn. Improve. Repeat.**

The framework turns commitments, decisions, risks, opportunities, and lessons into structured records and review points. It treats work, leadership, family, health, finances, and personal growth as connected operating concerns.

## Decision inputs

HELI-OS asks a decision to distinguish facts from assumptions and consider risk, impact, urgency, importance, long-term value, opportunity cost, health, family, finances, career, business, confidence, protected boundaries, next action, and review date.

The framework is intended to improve judgment. It does not make important life decisions for the owner.

## AI governance

- Evidence before confidence.
- Fact before inference.
- Read before write.
- Least privilege.
- Human approval for sensitive action.
- Privacy and security as design constraints.
- Auditability for consequential operations.
- Capability is not authority.

AI may summarize, analyze, research, draft, identify patterns, and recommend next actions. Those abilities do not grant permission to change external systems or override protected boundaries.

## Current status

These are documented operating principles and workflows. They are not implemented as an autonomous decision engine.
