# HELI-OS Portfolio Evidence

## What the repository demonstrates

- Systems thinking across connected personal and executive domains.
- Explicit separation of facts, assumptions, risks, priorities, and protected boundaries.
- Practical AI governance based on least privilege, approval, privacy, and auditability.
- Portable PowerShell procedures using repository-relative paths.
- A self-check and clean-copy portability test.
- A continuous-improvement model that turns lessons into workflows and safeguards.

## What it does not demonstrate yet

- A finished application platform.
- Live connector integrations.
- Autonomous execution.
- Production-scale telemetry or business outcome measurement.

The evidence is strongest as an operating-model and governance foundation.
